Physical model

G(E) = cos(αa) + [(β²−α²)/(2αβ)] sin(αa) tanh(βb)

s(E) = sech(βb)

Allowed when |G(E)| ≤ s(E)

α = √(mE/C₀), β = √(m(V₀−E)/C₀), C₀ = 0.0380998212 eV·nm². Equal effective mass in well and barrier. Energy is referenced to the GaAs well edge.
